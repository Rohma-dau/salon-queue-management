# Salon Queue — Project Brief & Scope

## Problem
Customers can spend a long time waiting without knowing when their turn will come. Manual queues can create confusion about arrival order and barber preferences. This app gives customers a clear live place in line and gives staff one shared queue.

## Users
- Customer: joins remotely, selects services and a preferred barber, tracks their token, and can cancel while waiting.
- Receptionist: adds walk-ins, calls eligible customers, and handles no-shows/cancellations.
- Barber: sees their assigned work, starts and completes a service, and changes availability.
- Owner: manages services, prices, barbers on duty, online joining, and daily statistics.

## Must-have features
1. Mobile-first home page with live queue and barber availability.
2. Customer join form with name, validated 10-digit phone, up to three services, and barber choice.
3. Unique daily token and live status with position and estimated wait.
4. Reception dashboard with walk-ins, barber cards, call-next, no-show and cancellation actions.
5. Barber workspace with current/next customer, start/done and break controls.
6. Waiting-area display for customers in chairs, called customers and next five waiting tokens.
7. Owner settings for services, barbers, queue opening and salon closing time, plus today's stats.
8. English/Gujarati interface labels and automatic cross-screen updates.

## Don't build
Online payments, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp notifications, loyalty points, customer accounts, or multiple salon branches.

## Look & feel
Premium salon atmosphere: charcoal (#1F2937-inspired dark charcoal) and warm gold (#D4A017), clean typography, rounded/contained cards, mobile-first layouts, and English/Gujarati labels.
